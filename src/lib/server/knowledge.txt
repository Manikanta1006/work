COMPANY OVERVIEW
====================================================================
INDQ Studio (INDQ = "Innovation. Design. Quotient.") is a human-centered
MedTech product design studio based in India. INDQ designs and develops
medical devices and connected health products end to end — from early
scientific research through to pre-production and manufacturing.

INDQ is a multidisciplinary team of Industrial Designers, Biomedical
Engineers, Hardware & Software Engineers, Doctors, and Manufacturing &
Regulatory Consultants. The studio works across Medical Electronics,
Medical Robotics, AI/ML-intensive products for Diagnostics & Therapeutics,
Industry 4.0, and Consumer Electronics.

Tagline lines used on the site: "Shape possibilities with us!" and
"Shaping Possibilities." The design philosophy keeps minimalism at its core.

Vision: Pioneering smart product solutions that integrate technology with
everyday needs, making advanced design accessible across industries worldwide.

DESIGN PROCESS  (Discover -> Develop -> Deliver)
====================================================================
DISCOVER
- Context Research (checks actionability): ethnography, task flow & error
  analysis, behaviour & cognition, competitive study.
- Technological Research (checks feasibility): possible technologies,
  advancements, algorithms, mechanisms.
- User Needs Mapping (checks desirability): stakeholder interviews,
  empathy mapping, value proposition mapping.

DEVELOP
- Proof of Concept (checks functionality): core features, unique selling
  proposition, embedded systems.
- Functional Prototype (checks viability): product conceptualisation and
  visualisation, rapid prototyping, minimum viable product, ergonomics.
- Advanced Prototype (checks customisability): user-centred design, PCB
  customisation, software integration, branding & story.

DELIVER
- Production Ready (checks manufacturability): design for assembly, design
  for manufacturing, process optimisation, cost reduction, batch
  production, packaging.
- Design for Mass Manufacturing (checks scalability): material selection,
  industrial process selection, packaging.
- Pre-Compliance & Testing (checks reliability): pre-compliance testing,
  functional testing, certification alignment (CE, FDA, etc.), risk
  analysis, documentation for QA/QC.

WORKING WITH INDQ (ENGAGEMENT)

To start a project, discuss services, collaborate, or ask about careers,
use the Customer Requirement Specification (CRS) form at

project type, technology domain, tools, deliverables, timeline,
milestones, point of contact, stakeholders, existing solutions,
acceptance criteria, plus your name and email.

Organization stages listed: Ideation / Concept; Seed / Early Stage;
Growth Stage; Established Business; Enterprise.
Typical project timeline options: 3-6 months, 6-12 months.

INDQ does not publish fixed prices. Cost and timeline depend on the
project scope and are discussed after an enquiry. Do not quote or
estimate prices.
